About MOVIES WORLD

MOVIES WORLD is a movie discovery and review platform covering films released from 2000 to 2026. Discover new favorites, read original reviews, and find where to watch legally.

Our Mission

We believe great movies deserve great curation. Every review on MOVIES WORLD is written by our editors — original, honest, and spoiler-conscious. We never host or link to pirated content; every "watch" button leads to an official, licensed platform.

Our Data

Movie metadata, posters and trailers are provided by The Movie Database (TMDB), a community-built, properly licensed movie database covering hundreds of thousands of titles. Ratings reflect TMDB community votes.

Legal & Copyright

MOVIES WORLD does not store, host or distribute any video files. Streaming, rental and purchase links direct you to official services such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV and Google TV. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
