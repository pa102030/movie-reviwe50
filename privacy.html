Privacy Policy

How MOVIES WORLD handles information, cookies, accounts and third-party services.

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MOVIES WORLD ("we", "us" or "our"), available at themoviesworld.com, may collect, use, store and share information when you visit or use the website. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

Some features and service providers may change as the website develops. We will update this page when our data practices materially change.

2. Information we may collect

Information you provide

If account registration, sign-in, a contact form or another interactive feature is enabled and you use it, we may receive information you submit, such as your email address, display name and the contents of a message. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information in a contact message.

Account and authentication information

Where Firebase Authentication is enabled, Google Firebase processes account and authentication information needed to create and maintain your sign-in session. This may include an account identifier, email address, display name (if provided), authentication provider, and technical sign-in/security records. We use this information to provide account-related features and protect the service. A Firebase project configuration value in website code is not, by itself, proof that every visitor has an account or that a separate database stores their profile.

Watchlist and preferences

Some site preferences, such as watchlist items, favourites or theme/language choices, may be stored in your browser or device using local storage or cookies, depending on the feature implementation. Browser-stored information may remain on that device until you clear it. If a feature is later connected to an account or cloud database, the relevant information may instead be processed by that service.

Automatically collected technical information

When you visit the website, your browser and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, approximate usage time, referring page, diagnostic logs and cookie identifiers. This information can be used for security, analytics, troubleshooting and advertising, where those services are enabled.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website and its features.
  • To create and manage user sign-in where account registration is enabled.
  • To remember preferences and locally saved watchlist/favourite selections.
  • To respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • To protect the website, detect abuse and troubleshoot errors.
  • To measure website performance and display advertising where enabled.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

MOVIES WORLD may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for site functionality, language preferences, security, measurement and advertising. Third-party services may also set or read cookies or use similar technologies through the website.

Where Google AdSense is used, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertisements, subject to your location, consent choices and Google's settings. These technologies may use information about visits to this and other websites to provide advertising. The availability and type of personalised advertising may vary by region and user settings.

You can review or change Google's advertising personalisation settings at Google Ads Settings. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features. For information about how Google uses data on partner sites, see Google's partner-site data information.

Where applicable law requires consent before non-essential cookies or personalised advertising are used, the relevant consent mechanism should be presented and respected. Browser settings alone may not replace a legally required consent choice.

5. Third-party services

Google Firebase

We may use Firebase Authentication and related Google Firebase services for account sign-in, authentication, security and service operation. Information handled by Firebase is subject to Firebase's privacy information and Google's applicable terms.

TMDB

Movie titles, descriptions, ratings, posters, backdrops, cast information and other movie metadata may be requested from The Movie Database (TMDB). When your browser loads this content, it may communicate with TMDB and your request may expose technical information such as your IP address to that service. TMDB handles information under its own policies. See TMDB's Privacy Policy.

Google Translate and external links

If the translation feature is used, your browser may load Google Translate resources and set translation-related cookies. Links to streaming platforms, social networks or other external websites take you away from MOVIES WORLD. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms; we do not control their data practices.

6. How information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information may be processed by service providers that help us host, secure, authenticate, translate, measure or advertise on the website, including Google/Firebase and TMDB where relevant. We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users or protect our rights and the website.

7. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Retention periods can differ between browser storage, authentication providers, server logs and third-party services.

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for using a secure device.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage browser cookies and local storage through your browser settings. If you have an account and want to ask about access, correction or deletion of account information, contact us through the Contact page. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained for security, legal or legitimate operational reasons.

Deleting browser data may remove locally stored watchlists or preferences. It does not necessarily delete information held by Firebase or another third-party provider; those requests may need to be handled through the relevant account or service.

9. Children's privacy

MOVIES WORLD is a general movie information and review website and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us through our Contact page so we can review the request.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection laws in those countries may differ. Where required, applicable service providers are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for their processing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website, its features or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when this page was most recently revised. Continued use of the website after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the updated policy; it does not override rights you may have under applicable law.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, please use the MOVIES WORLD Contact page. Include enough detail for us to understand your request, but do not send your password or other sensitive login credentials.
